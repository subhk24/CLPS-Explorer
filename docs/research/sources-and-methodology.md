## 1. Data Governance & Methodology Principles

The CLPS Explorer database was compiled under five core research guidelines to ensure absolute academic, scientific, and journalistic defensibility:

1. **Primary Sources Exclusively:**  
   Every mission status, coordinate, payload acronym, lead center, and contract value was extracted directly from primary NASA web pages, NASA press kits, NASA Office of Inspector General (OIG) reports, peer-reviewed instrument papers, or SEC regulatory filings by commercial contractors. No third-party blog speculation or unverified social media claims were incorporated.
2. **Clear Demarcation of Official Facts vs. Uncertain Future Plans:**  
   Past missions are recorded with their verified historical outcomes (e.g., flight anomalies, actual coordinates confirmed by LROC). Future missions are explicitly marked as "Planned" with dates clearly identified as "Target Windows."
3. **Strict Separation of NASA Payloads vs. Commercial Co-Manifests:**  
   NASA’s "60+ payloads" metric strictly counts NASA-funded science and technology investigations awarded across the 17 commercial task orders. Commercial co-manifests (e.g., commercial rovers, art projects, memorial capsules) are noted where relevant for mission context but excluded from NASA science inventory counts.
4. **Selenographic Datum Integrity:**  
   Landing coordinates are mapped to the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) standard geodetic reference grid. For past landed missions, coordinates represent LROC-verified touchdown points; for planned missions, coordinates represent NASA’s documented center-point targets.
5. **Independent Challenge Disclaimer:**  
   CLPS Explorer is an independent, non-commercial research and visualization project built for the NASA Challenge 2026. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or officially endorsed by the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

## 2. Status Classification Standards

To eliminate ambiguity, CLPS Explorer assigns one of six standardized operational statuses to every mission:

| Status Identifier | Formal Definition | Historical / Current Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Landed (Nominal)** | Spacecraft completed powered descent, touched down upright, and operated through nominal surface duration. | *Surveyor heritage; target for future nominal CLPS flights.* |
| **Landed (Anomaly / Tilt)** | Spacecraft achieved soft touchdown on the lunar surface and successfully operated NASA payloads, but experienced a structural or orientation anomaly (e.g., fractured strut, lateral tilt). | **Intuitive Machines IM-1 (Odysseus)** at Malapert A (Feb 2024). |
| **Transit Loss / Re-entry** | Spacecraft launched into space but suffered a propulsion or systems anomaly in transit that prevented a lunar landing attempt, resulting in a controlled atmospheric re-entry or loss. | **Astrobotic Peregrine (TO2-AB)** (Jan 2024). |
| **Delivered / Surface Active** | Landed on the lunar surface and executing active surface science investigations during lunar day. | **Firefly Blue Ghost 1 (TO19D)** at Mare Crisium. |
| **Planned (On Contract)** | Task order competitively awarded to a specific commercial vendor with defined payload manifest; in assembly or testing. | **PRIME-1 (IM-2)**, **CP-11 (IM-3)**, **CP-12 (Draper)**, **CS-3/4 (BG2)**. |
| **Flight Demonstration / Test** | Contract modified or maintained to validate lander propulsion and avionics using mass simulators rather than prime rovers. | **Astrobotic Griffin 1 (TO20A)**. |
